Your Lock Screen becomes your notes.

Vellure shows your notes on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island as iOS Live Activities. Without opening the app, today's tasks, the days left until a date, and your progress are always in view.

■ Made for you if
- You keep forgetting notes because opening an app is a hassle
- You want a D-day or deadline front and center
- You'd like to tick off tasks right from the Lock Screen

■ 5 note types
- Plain: a short note on your Lock Screen
- Checklist: check items right from the Lock Screen
- D-day: automatic count of days left until a date
- Countdown: live countdown to a target time
- Progress: adjust a progress bar from the Lock Screen

■ Notes that tidy themselves
Keep them pinned, or have them disappear automatically when the target time arrives, a set time passes, everything is checked, or you hit 100%.

■ Siri & Shortcuts
Say something like "Vellure note, buy milk" and it's saved and posted without opening the app. It also works with location and time automations.

■ Privacy first
No sign-up. Every note is stored only on this device. Nothing is sent to a server, and deleting the app deletes your data.

Completely free. Put your first note on the Lock Screen now.

* Due to iOS, a Live Activity is shown for up to 12 hours after posting, then ends automatically.
